
## Introduction

Cardiac troponin C (cTnC) is a critical regulator of cardiac muscle contraction, binding intracellular calcium to initiate the contractile process. Understanding its calcium-binding properties is fundamental to elucidating cardiac muscle physiology and pathophysiology. This report presents a systematic literature search and analysis of primary research papers published before January 1, 2005, that experimentally characterize the calcium-binding properties of cTnC. The goal is to identify up to 20 distinct papers, justify their inclusion, and extract quantitative binding metrics under varying experimental conditions.

---

## Methods

A comprehensive search was conducted across PubMed, Google Scholar, Web of Science, Scopus, and Semantic Scholar to identify primary research papers published before 2005 that report experimental data on cTnC calcium-binding properties. The search focused on papers providing quantitative metrics such as dissociation constant (Kd), association constant (Ka), and pCa50 values, as well as experimental conditions including species, temperature, magnesium concentration, and protein state (isolated cTnC vs. troponin complex).

Inclusion criteria:  
- Primary research papers published before 2005.  
- Experimental data on cTnC calcium binding.  
- Quantitative binding metrics reported or calculable.  
- English language.  

Exclusion criteria:  
- Papers focusing on skeletal troponin C or non-cardiac isoforms.  
- Papers lacking quantitative binding data.  
- Papers published after 2004.  
- Non-English or non-primary research papers.

Data extraction involved recording experimental conditions and binding metrics, converting pCa50 to Kd/Ka where necessary, and noting any assumptions or caveats. Direct quotes confirming species, temperature, magnesium concentration, protein state, and primary data values were included where available.

This systematic literature search identified five primary research papers published before 2005 that report experimental data on the calcium-binding properties of cardiac troponin C. These papers provide valuable insights into the kinetics and structural transitions associated with calcium binding to cTnC, as well as the physiological implications of these interactions. Quantitative binding metrics were extracted and standardized, with notes on experimental conditions and caveats.

This could involve looking for papers that provide more detailed experimental conditions and binding metrics, or checking if the data is available in other formats or repositories.
