# Calcium-Binding Properties of Cardiac Troponin C (cTnC): A Pre-2005 Primary-Literature Dataset

## TL;DR
- A blind, independent search identified **21 qualifying pre-2005 primary research papers** reporting experimentally derived Ca²⁺-binding constants for cardiac TnC; the field's quantitative core was built by the Potter/Solaro, Kay/McCubbin, Putkey, Sykes, Tibbits/Gillis, and Davis/Tikunova groups.
- The data confirm a **two-tier affinity architecture**: structural sites III/IV bind Ca²⁺ with Ka ≈ 2×10⁷–3×10⁸ M⁻¹ (Kd ≈ 0.003–0.5 µM) and compete with Mg²⁺, while the single regulatory site II binds with Ka ≈ 4×10⁴–4.5×10⁵ M⁻¹ (Kd ≈ 2–24 µM) and is essentially Mg²⁺-insensitive (Mg²⁺ Kd ≈ 4 mM at the regulatory site; Johnson et al. 1980).
- **Magnesium and protein context are the dominant modifiers**: in whole bovine troponin 4 mM Mg²⁺ lowers the high-affinity-site Ka ~10-fold (3×10⁸ → 3×10⁷ M⁻¹; Holroyde et al. 1980), and the low-affinity regulatory site binds ~10-fold more tightly in whole troponin (Ka 2×10⁶ M⁻¹) than in isolated cTnC (Ka 2×10⁵ M⁻¹).

## Key Findings
1. **Site II (regulatory, low-affinity) constants cluster at Kd ≈ 2–24 µM**, varying with protein state and Mg²⁺. Isolated cTnC: Kd ≈ 2–8 µM (Hazard 1998, IAANS; Tikunova & Davis 2004, F27W ≈ 7 µM apo); whole troponin: Kd ≈ 0.2–0.5 µM (Li 2002; Holroyde 1980).
2. **Sites III/IV (structural, high-affinity) constants cluster at Ka ≈ 2×10⁷–3×10⁸ M⁻¹** and are reduced ~10-fold in whole troponin (Holroyde 1980) or ~2-fold in isolated cTnC (Burtnick & Kay 1977) by physiological Mg²⁺, which binds these sites competitively (Mg²⁺ Ka ≈ 0.7–3×10³ M⁻¹).
3. **Temperature dependence** of site II affinity is modest for mammalian cTnC but pronounced for cold-adapted salmonid cTnC: cooling 37→21→7°C dropped bovine K1/2 by only 0.13 then 0.32 pCa units, but trout K1/2 by 0.76 then 0.42 (Gillis 2000).
4. **Methodological split**: pre-1990 work used equilibrium/flow dialysis (⁴⁵Ca) and CD/UV/fluorescence on purified proteins or skinned fibers; post-1990 work used recombinant Trp (F27W) or IAANS fluorescence probes and NMR titration.
5. Reported affinities depend strongly on whether the value is an "intrinsic" site II constant or a global/combined value — hence the added Site column to prevent conflation of constants differing by 1–2 orders of magnitude.

### 1. Burtnick & Kay (1977)
- **Citation:** Burtnick, L. D., & Kay, C. M. (1977). The calcium-binding properties of bovine cardiac troponin C. *FEBS Letters, 75*(1), 105–110.
- **Relevance:** Among the first direct determinations of Ca²⁺ binding constants for purified bovine cardiac TnC; reports native-protein association constants with and without Mg²⁺.
- **Methodology Note:** Flow/equilibrium dialysis with ⁴⁵Ca and spectroscopic monitoring.
- **Flags/Caveats:** Kd values calculated by inversion of reported Ka. The native cTnC values (5.9×10⁷, 2.9×10⁷ M⁻¹) are quoted verbatim in the companion C-terminal-peptide paper (PMC1153847); confirm against the FEBS original. This isolated-cTnC ~2-fold Mg²⁺ reduction is smaller than the ~10-fold seen in whole troponin (Holroyde 1980).

## Confidence Assessment
- **Search completeness: Medium-High.** All major research lineages (Potter/Solaro/Johnson; Kay/McCubbin/Hincke; Putkey/Dotson; Sykes/Li/Spyracopoulos; Tibbits/Gillis; Davis/Tikunova; Hofmann/Fuchs; Falke/Hazard) were recovered independently via PubMed and full-text/abstract web search, with cross-checking against open-access reference lists. - **Data reliability:** Association/dissociation constants for sites III/IV and the headline site II values are high-confidence (verbatim from abstracts/full text). Inferred or cross-sourced values are explicitly flagged in the relevant rows (Gillis 2000 Kd; Tikunova 2004 24-µM Mg²⁺ value).
- **Data gaps:** (1) Several absolute affinities are reported only graphically (Gillis F27W series); (2) buffer Mg²⁺ is frequently unstated for isolated-protein studies (marked "Assumed 0 mM" where a metal-free/EGTA buffer is implied, "not verified" where a lab-standard Mg²⁺ is likely); (3) experimental temperature is often unstated for room-temperature dialysis work (marked "~22 assumed"); (4) post-1990 fluorescence studies were typically run at 15°C (Davis/Tikunova lab standard), marked "assumed" where not explicitly recovered.
