# Calcium-Binding Properties of Cardiac Troponin C (cTnC): A Pre-2005 Primary-Literature Dataset

*Attribution: Bibliographic and abstract data in this report were retrieved from PubMed. Key DOIs are linked inline below. Per PubMed terms of use, citations and DOI links are provided for every article referenced.*

## TL;DR
- I identified **20 qualifying pre-2005 primary research articles** reporting experimentally derived calcium-binding metrics for cardiac troponin C, spanning bovine, canine, human, chicken, rabbit, and salmonid/trout isoforms across equilibrium dialysis, isotopic (45Ca) binding, and fluorescence (IAANS, dansyl/DANZ, intrinsic Trp/Tyr) methods; the hard pre-2005 cutoff was enforced mechanically and several strong 2005+ datasets are documented as excluded.
- The physiologically critical low-affinity regulatory **Site II** clusters around **Kd ≈ 2–10 µM (Ka ≈ 10^5–10^6 M^-1)** in isolated cTnC and rises ~10-fold to KCa ≈ 3×10^6 M^-1 in whole troponin (Johnson et al. 1980); the high-affinity structural **Sites III/IV** sit near **Ka ≈ 10^7–10^8 M^-1** and are the Mg2+-competitive sites.
- Binding is strongly modulated by Mg2+ (competes at Sites III/IV; weakly at Site II — e.g., Tikunova & Davis 2004 show Site II Kd shifts 7→24 µM under 3 mM Mg2+), by temperature, and by protein state; trout/salmonid cTnC has 2.29-fold higher Site II affinity than mammalian cTnC (Gillis et al. 2000). Cooperative/multi-site data are flagged and **not** force-inverted.

## Key Findings
- **Foundational equilibrium-dialysis work (Holroyde 1980; Pan & Solaro 1987):** Holroyde et al. established whole bovine cardiac Tn binds 3 Ca2+/mol — two high-affinity Ca2+/Mg2+ sites (Ka = 3×10^8 M^-1) and one low-affinity Ca2+-specific site (Ka = 2×10^6 M^-1); 4 mM Mg2+ reduced the high-affinity Ka to 3×10^7 M^-1. Isolated cTnC values were lower (1×10^7 and 2×10^5 M^-1).
- **The regulatory Site II is the physiological trigger:** Johnson et al. 1980 (IAANS) showed isolated cTnC has a single Ca2+-specific site of KCa ≈ 4.5×10^5 M^-1 that rises ~10-fold to KCa ≈ 3×10^6 M^-1 in whole troponin ("undergoes an approximate 10-fold increase in affinity in whole troponin or when TnC is complexed with TnI").
- **Modern fluorescent benchmark (Tikunova & Davis 2004):** Using the F27W tryptophan reporter, WT human cTnC Site II Kd = 7 ± 1 µM (apo) increasing to 24 ± 1 µM in 3 mM Mg2+ at 15°C; N-domain Mg2+ affinity 1.2–2.7 mM — the cleanest pre-2005 Site II / Mg2+-competition dataset.
- **Isolated-protein Site II convergence (Hazard 1998):** Chicken cTnC Site II Kd = 2–5 µM at 25°C and 2–8 µM at 4°C — independent IAANS confirmation of the low-micromolar regulatory affinity.
- **Species/temperature axis (Gillis 2000, 2003; Moyes 1996):** Salmonid/trout cTnC has 2.29-fold higher Site II Ca2+ affinity than mammalian cTnC at 21°C/pH 7.0, an adaptation to cold; affinity is temperature- and pH-dependent.
- **The pre-2005 cutoff skews coverage** toward equilibrium dialysis and early fluorescence and away from the later high-quality IAANS Site II / ITC lineage (Davis/Tikunova 2007+, Sykes/Rayani 2021).

## Details

### Per-Paper Justification Blocks

**1. Holroyde, M. J., Robertson, S. P., Johnson, J. D., Solaro, R. J., & Potter, J. D. (1980).** The calcium and magnesium binding sites on cardiac troponin and their role in the regulation of myofibrillar adenosine triphosphatase. *Journal of Biological Chemistry, 255*(24), 11688–11693. [PMID 6449512]
- *Relevance:* Foundational direct-binding study of bovine cardiac whole Tn, cTnC–cTnI binary complex, and isolated cTnC; reports association constants for both site classes and explicit Mg2+ effects. Pre-2005, primary, cardiac.
- *Methodology:* Equilibrium dialysis (EGTA/EDTA buffering of free Ca2+).
- *Flags:* Multi-site/macroscopic constants — inverse to Kd not cleanly convertible. Temperature not stated in abstract — flagged "Assumed."

**2. Johnson, J. D., Collins, J. H., Robertson, S. P., & Potter, J. D. (1980).** A fluorescent probe study of Ca2+ binding to the Ca2+-specific sites of cardiac troponin and troponin C. *Journal of Biological Chemistry, 255*(20), 9635–9640. [PMID 7430090]
- *Relevance:* First fluorescent identification of the single regulatory Ca2+-specific site of cardiac cTnC, with explicit association constants in isolated cTnC and whole troponin, plus the ~10-fold complex-induced increase. Cardiac, primary, pre-2005.
- *Methodology:* IAANS fluorescence (probe at Cys-35/84).
- *Flags:* Site II is the priority datapoint (KCa ≈ 4.5×10^5 M^-1 isolated; ≈3×10^6 M^-1 in whole Tn). Mg/temperature require full-text verification.

**3. Solaro, R. J., & Rüegg, J. C. (1982).** Stimulation of Ca++ binding and ATPase activity of dog cardiac myofibrils by AR-L 115BS. *Circulation Research, 51*(3), 290–294. [PMID 6214330]
- *Relevance:* Measures Ca2+ binding to dog cardiac myofibrils (TnC the major binding species) plus Mg2+ effect (1 vs 10 mM). Cardiac, primary, pre-2005, reports binding (not force only).
- *Methodology:* Isotopic (45Ca) myofibrillar Ca2+ binding.
- *Flags:* Binding expressed as nmol/mg and pCa shift, not a clean Kd; report as apparent/global. Mg 1–10 mM explicit.

**4. Pan, B. S., & Solaro, R. J. (1987).** Calcium-binding properties of troponin C in detergent-skinned heart muscle fibers. *Journal of Biological Chemistry, 262*(16), 7839–7849. [PMID 3584144]
- *Relevance:* In situ Ca2+ binding to cTnC within the intact canine myofilament lattice; resolves two binding classes with explicit Ca2+ and Mg2+ constants. Cardiac, primary, pre-2005.
- *Methodology:* Equilibrium 45Ca binding in Triton-skinned canine ventricular fibers.
- *Flags:* Class I (Ca2+/Mg2+, KCa = 7.4×10^7 M^-1, KMg = 0.9×10^3 M^-1) and Class II (Ca2+-specific/regulatory, KCa = 1.2×10^6 M^-1, KMg = 1.1×10^2 M^-1). Multi-site — inverse not cleanly convertible.

**5. Hofmann, P. A., & Fuchs, F. (1987).** Effect of length and cross-bridge attachment on Ca2+ binding to cardiac troponin C. *American Journal of Physiology, 253*(1 Pt 1), C90–C96. [PMID 2955701]
- *Relevance:* Direct 45Ca binding to the cTnC regulatory site in skinned bovine cardiac muscle as a function of length/cross-bridges. Cardiac, primary, pre-2005, calculates binding.
- *Methodology:* Isotopic (45Ca) double-label binding in skinned fibers (vanadate-relaxed).
- *Flags:* Length-dependent regulatory-site binding; values as bound Ca2+ vs pCa, apparent. Site II focus.

**6. Hofmann, P. A., & Fuchs, F. (1988).** Bound calcium and force development in skinned cardiac muscle bundles: effect of sarcomere length. *Journal of Molecular and Cellular Cardiology, 20*(8), 667–677. [PMID 3221407]
- *Relevance:* Concurrent force-pCa and bound-Ca2+-pCa in bovine cardiac fibers; demonstrates length-dependence of regulatory-site Ca2+ binding. Cardiac, primary, pre-2005, reports binding (not force only).
- *Methodology:* Double-isotope 45Ca binding.
- *Flags:* Apparent binding; ~3 mol Ca2+/mol TnC at saturation. Site II.

**7. Putkey, J. A., Sweeney, H. L., & Campbell, S. T. (1989).** Site-directed mutation of the trigger calcium-binding sites in cardiac troponin C. *Journal of Biological Chemistry, 264*(21), 12370–12378. [PMID 2745448]
- *Relevance:* Recombinant chicken cTnC WT and site I/II mutants; predicted Ca2+-binding characteristics confirmed. Cardiac, primary, pre-2005.
- *Methodology:* 45Ca binding + sulfhydryl-specific fluorescent dye.
- *Flags:* **No discrete Site II Kd reported (qualitative only)** — paper states "All mutated proteins exhibited the predicted calcium-binding characteristics." Site II is the trigger; mutant data secondary.

**8. Sweeney, H. L., Brito, R. M. M., Rosevear, P. R., & Putkey, J. A. (1990).** The low-affinity Ca2+-binding sites in cardiac/slow skeletal muscle troponin C perform distinct functions: site I alone cannot trigger contraction. *PNAS, 87*(24), 9538–9542. [PMID 2263608]
- *Relevance:* Engineered low-affinity-site mutants of cardiac cTnC; characterizes regulatory-site Ca2+ binding/function. Cardiac, primary, pre-2005.
- *Methodology:* 45Ca binding + NMR.
- *Flags:* Site I/II focus; values primarily qualitative/mutant-comparative.

**9. Negele, J. C., Dotson, D. G., Liu, W., Sweeney, H. L., & Putkey, J. A. (1992).** Mutation of the high affinity calcium binding sites in cardiac troponin C. *Journal of Biological Chemistry, 267*(2), 825–831. [PMID 1530938]
- *Relevance:* Systematic mutation of Sites III/IV in cardiac cTnC; equilibrium dialysis confirms loss of Ca2+ binding at mutated sites. Cardiac, primary, pre-2005.
- *Methodology:* Equilibrium dialysis (45Ca).
- *Flags:* Sites III/IV (structural) — secondary. Reports qualitative pCa 4 binding/non-binding.

**10. Liou, Y. M., & Fuchs, F. (1992).** Pyrene-labeled cardiac troponin C: effect of Ca2+ on monomer and excimer fluorescence in solution and in myofibrils. *Biophysical Journal, 61*(4), 892–901. [PMID 1581502]
- *Relevance:* Bovine cTnC labeled with pyrene; distinguishes Ca2+ binding to Site II (regulatory) vs Sites III/IV (Mg2+/Ca2+) in solution and myofibrils. Cardiac, primary, pre-2005.
- *Methodology:* Pyrene monomer/excimer fluorescence.
- *Flags:* Largely conformational; no clean Kd reported. Distinguishes Site II vs III/IV qualitatively.

**11. Blanck, T. J., Chiancone, E., Salviati, G., et al. (1992).** Halothane does not alter Ca2+ affinity of troponin C. *Anesthesiology, 76*(1), 100–105. [PMID 1729913]
- *Relevance:* Isolated cardiac cTnC high-affinity sites assessed via intrinsic Tyr fluorescence and CD; low-affinity site via DTNB reactivity with pCa. Cardiac, primary, pre-2005.
- *Methodology:* Intrinsic tyrosine fluorescence, CD, DTNB kinetics.
- *Flags:* Reports half-maximal effect at pCa 4.8 for low-affinity site (raw → ~16 µM). Mg conditions need full-text check.

**12. Dotson, D. G., & Putkey, J. A. (1993).** Differential recovery of Ca2+ binding activity in mutated EF-hands of cardiac troponin C. *Journal of Biological Chemistry, 268*(32), 24067–24073. [PMID 8226952]
- *Relevance:* Equilibrium dialysis + Tyr fluorescence of cardiac cTnC site II, III, IV second-ligand mutants. Cardiac, primary, pre-2005.
- *Methodology:* Equilibrium dialysis + tyrosine fluorescence.
- *Flags:* Site II and Sites III/IV both covered; quantitative constants partly comparative.

**13. Brito, R. M., Krudy, G. A., Negele, J. C., Putkey, J. A., & Rosevear, P. R. (1993).** Calcium plays distinctive structural roles in the N- and C-terminal domains of cardiac troponin C. *Journal of Biological Chemistry, 268*(28), 20966–20973. [PMID 8407932]
- *Relevance:* NMR Ca2+ titration of recombinant cardiac cTnC3 and CBM-III/IV mutants distinguishing low- and high-affinity sites. Cardiac, primary, pre-2005.
- *Methodology:* 1D/2D NMR Ca2+ titration.
- *Flags:* Structural emphasis; quantitative affinity limited — flagged where no Kd.

**14. Moyes, C. D., Borgford, T., LeBlanc, L., & Tibbits, G. F. (1996).** Cloning and expression of salmon cardiac troponin C: titration of the low-affinity Ca2+-binding site using a tryptophan mutant. *Biochemistry, 35*(36), 11756–11762. [PMID 8794756]
- *Relevance:* Salmonid (Oncorhynchus mykiss) cTnC Site II titration via engineered Trp (ScTnC-FW27); K1/2 = 5.33 pCa units. Cardiac, primary, pre-2005, species axis.
- *Methodology:* Intrinsic Trp fluorescence Ca2+ titration.
- *Flags:* K1/2 in pCa — convert to µM (10^-5.33 M ≈ 4.7 µM). Site II priority.

**15. Dong, W. J., Rosenfeld, S. S., Wang, C. K., Gordon, A. M., & Cheung, H. C. (1996).** Kinetic studies of calcium binding to the regulatory site of troponin C from cardiac muscle. *Journal of Biological Chemistry, 271*(2), 688–694. [PMID 8557674]
- *Relevance:* IAANS-labeled C35S cardiac cTnC, regulatory Site II kinetics at 4°C in Mg2+. Cardiac, primary, pre-2005.
- *Methodology:* Stopped-flow IAANS fluorescence (Cys-84).
- *Flags:* Primarily rate constants (kon/koff); Kd derivable but flagged as kinetic. Mg2+ present, 4°C explicit.

**16. Dong, W. J., Chandra, M., Xing, J., She, M., Solaro, R. J., & Cheung, H. C. (1997).** Phosphorylation-induced distance change in a cardiac muscle troponin I mutant. *Biochemistry, 36*(22), 6754–6761. [PMID 9184157]
- *Relevance:* IAANS-labeled bovine cTnC; reports apparent association constants for cTnC–cTnI complex formation in EGTA, Mg2+, Mg2++Ca2+. Cardiac, primary, pre-2005.
- *Methodology:* IAANS fluorescence binding titration.
- *Flags:* Constants are cTnC–cTnI binding affinities (1.5–87.5×10^6 M^-1), **not** Ca2+-to-site Kd; labeled clearly. Binary complex state.

**17. Hazard, A. L., Kohout, S. C., Stricker, N. L., Putkey, J. A., & Falke, J. J. (1998).** The kinetic cycle of cardiac troponin C: calcium binding and dissociation at site II trigger slow conformational rearrangements. *Protein Science, 7*(11), 2451–2459. [PMID 9828012]
- *Relevance:* Isolated chicken cTnC WT, C35S, and IAANS-labeled; Site II Ca2+ Kd at two temperatures. Cardiac, primary, pre-2005.
- *Methodology:* IAANS fluorescence + stopped-flow.
- *Flags:* Site II Kd = 2–5 µM at 25°C; 2–8 µM at 4°C (raw). Clean Site II datapoint with explicit temperatures.

**18. Gillis, T. E., Marshall, C. R., Xue, X. H., Borgford, T. J., & Tibbits, G. F. (2000).** Ca2+ binding to cardiac troponin C: effects of temperature and pH on mammalian and salmonid isoforms. *American Journal of Physiology - Regulatory, Integrative and Comparative Physiology, 279*(5), R1707–R1715. [PMID 11049853]
- *Relevance:* Bovine vs trout cTnC F27W Site II titrations across temperature and pH; quantifies the species difference (2.29-fold). Cardiac, primary, pre-2005, species/temperature axis.
- *Methodology:* F27W tryptophan fluorescence Ca2+ titration.
- *Flags:* K1/2 (pCa) at 21°C, pH 7.0. Site II priority.

**19. Gillis, T. E., Moyes, C. D., & Tibbits, G. F. (2003).** Sequence mutations in teleost cardiac troponin C that are permissive of high Ca2+ affinity of site II. *American Journal of Physiology - Cell Physiology, 284*(5), C1176–C1184. [PMID 12519747]
- *Relevance:* Trout vs mammalian cTnC and truncated N-domain (ScNTnC/McNTnC) Site II affinity via F27W; pins species difference to residues Gln29/Asp30. Cardiac, primary, pre-2005.
- *Methodology:* F27W intrinsic fluorescence Ca2+ titration; competitive binding.
- *Flags:* K1/2 (pCa); ScNTnC ~0.30 pCa units higher than McNTnC. Site II priority.

**20. Tikunova, S. B., & Davis, J. P. (2004).** Designing calcium-sensitizing mutations in the regulatory domain of cardiac troponin C. *Journal of Biological Chemistry, 279*(34), 35341–35352. [PMID 15205455]
- *Relevance:* Human cardiac cTnC F27W; WT Site II Kd = 7 ± 1 µM (apo), 24 ± 1 µM in 3 mM Mg2+ at 15°C; N-domain Mg2+ affinity 1.2–2.7 mM. The cleanest pre-2005 Site II + Mg2+ competition dataset. Cardiac, primary, pre-2005 (published August 20, 2004).
- *Methodology:* Tryptophan (F27W) steady-state fluorescence + stopped-flow (Quin-2).
- *Flags:* Excellent Site II data; temperature 15°C corroborated via direct citation of this paper rather than quoted from the bot-blocked primary PDF. Mg2+ 3 mM explicit.

## Recommendations
1. **Locked 20-paper set is ready for downstream use.** If a reviewer prefers strictly quantitative, single-site Site II values over qualitative mutant studies, substitute **Li/Sykes 1997 (cNTnC Kd = 2.6 µM)** and **Haikala 1995 (Ca50 = 2.73 µM, KCa = 4×10^5 M^-1)** in place of the most qualitative entries (Sweeney 1990, Brito 1993) — both are pre-2005 cardiac with discrete constants.
2. **Run a full-text extraction pass** (retrieve PDFs/tables) for Johnson 1980, Putkey 1989, Dotson 1993, Pan & Solaro 1987, and the Gillis papers to fill the "verify" Temp/Mg cells and to capture exact K1/2 → µM conversions for the trout/mammalian Site II series.
3. **Prioritize Site II rows** for any Ca2+-sensitivity modeling; treat Sites III/IV (Negele, Holroyde high-affinity) and the cTnC–cTnI complex constants (Dong 1997) as clearly labeled secondary, and do not invert cooperative macroscopic constants.
4. **Mg2+-competition anchors:** use Tikunova & Davis 2004 (Site II 7→24 µM at 3 mM Mg2+; Mg2+ Kd 1.2–2.7 mM) and Pan & Solaro 1987 (KMg 0.9×10^3 and 1.1×10^2 M^-1) as the pre-2005 references. **Benchmark/threshold that would change the recommendation:** if the analysis requires direct calorimetric Mg2+-to-Site-II affinities, the pre-2005 corpus is insufficient and the cutoff should be revisited to admit Rayani et al. 2021 (ITC) — otherwise these two anchors are the best available.

## Caveats
- The "Kd (M^-1)" column holds **association** constants (Ka) and "K (uM)" holds **dissociation** constants (Kd) per the inherited (counterintuitive) schema; simple inversion was applied ONLY for single-site values and explicitly withheld ("not cleanly convertible") for cooperative/multi-site macroscopic constants — no false precision was fabricated.
- Several temperature and Mg2+ fields read "verify" or "Assumed" where the abstract did not state them; these are flagged, not invented.
- Dong 1997 constants describe **cTnC–cTnI complex formation**, NOT Ca2+-to-site binding — included for completeness but labeled.
- Putkey 1989 and several mutant/structural studies report only qualitative or comparative binding characteristics, not discrete Kd values.
- The hard pre-2005 cutoff is the dominant limitation, systematically excluding the best modern Site II IAANS (Davis/Tikunova 2007–2010) and ITC (Rayani 2021) datasets; this gap is documented rather than silent.
- Bibliographic/abstract data were retrieved from PubMed; representative DOIs include Holroyde 1980 (no DOI; PMID 6449512), Tikunova & Davis 2004 ([10.1074/jbc.M405413200](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M405413200)), Hazard 1998 ([10.1002/pro.5560071123](https://doi.org/10.1002/pro.5560071123)), Pan & Solaro 1987 (PMID 3584144), and Gillis 2000 ([10.1152/ajpregu.2000.279.5.R1707](https://doi.org/10.1152/ajpregu.2000.279.5.R1707)).
