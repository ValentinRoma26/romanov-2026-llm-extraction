# Cardiac Troponin C Calcium Binding Kinetics: Systematic Literature Review (Pre-2005)

## Overview

This systematic review identified **15 primary research articles** published before 2005 that report quantitative calcium-binding data for cardiac troponin C (cTnC). The search encompassed multiple query strategies across Google Scholar, targeting equilibrium dialysis, fluorescence titration (IAANS, pyrene, intrinsic tryptophan), CD spectroscopy, and stopped-flow kinetic methods. Data were extracted for isolated cTnC, binary complexes (cTnC-cTnI), ternary troponin complexes, and reconstituted thin filaments across multiple species (bovine, chicken, human recombinant, rabbit, dog, salmonid), temperatures (4–37°C), and magnesium concentrations (0–10 mM).

## Paper Justification Blocks

### Paper 1: Holroyde et al. 1980
**Citation:** Holroyde, M.J., Robertson, S.P., Johnson, J.D., Solaro, R.J., & Potter, J.D. 1980. The calcium and magnesium binding sites on cardiac troponin and their role in the regulation of myofibrillar adenosine triphosphatase. *Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 25524, 11688–11693.
**Relevance:** This seminal paper provides equilibrium dialysis Ca²⁺ binding data for bovine cardiac TnC, TnI·TnC binary complex, reconstituted troponin, and native troponin under two Mg²⁺ conditions. It reports fitted Ka values and stoichiometries for high- and low-affinity sites ([1.1], [1.2], [1.3], [1.4], [1.5]).
**Methodology Note:** Equilibrium dialysis with ⁴⁵Ca; nonlinear least-squares fitting.
**Flags/Caveats:** Temperature stated as 4°C for dialysis. MgCl₂ conditions: 1 × 10⁻⁶ M (~0 effective) and 4 × 10⁻³ M.

### Paper 2: Stull & Buss 1978
**Citation:** Stull, J.T., & Buss, J.E. 1978. Calcium binding properties of beef cardiac troponin. *Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 25317, 5932–5938.
**Relevance:** Provides equilibrium dialysis and equilibrium filtration Ca²⁺ binding data for beef cardiac troponin at multiple MgCl₂ concentrations (0, 0.3, 2.0, 10 mM), reporting [Ca²⁺]₀.₅ values and association constants ([2.1], [2.2], [2.3], [2.4], [2.5], [2.6]).
**Methodology Note:** Equilibrium dialysis (EGTA/EDTA buffered) and Hummel-Dreyer gel filtration.
**Flags/Caveats:** Temperature not explicitly stated in accessible text. Whole troponin measured, not isolated cTnC.

### Paper 3: Burtnick & Kay 1977
**Citation:** Burtnick, L.D., & Kay, C.M. 1977. The calcium-binding properties of bovine cardiac troponin C. *FEBS Letters*, 75(1-2), 105–110.
**Relevance:** Reports CD-based Ca²⁺ titration of bovine cardiac TnC with and without 2 mM MgCl₂, yielding Ka values for isolated cTnC ([3.1], [3.2]).
**Methodology Note:** Circular dichroism titration at 221 nm; gel filtration for stoichiometry.
**Flags/Caveats:** Measurements at 22°C, pH 7.5. Ka = 7 × 10⁶ M⁻¹ (no Mg²⁺); Ka ~ 5 × 10⁴ M⁻¹ (2 mM Mg²⁺).

### Paper 4: Johnson et al. 1980
**Citation:** Johnson, J.D., Collins, J.H., Robertson, S.P., & Potter, J.D. 1980. A fluorescent probe study of Ca²⁺ binding to the Ca²⁺-specific sites of cardiac troponin and troponin C. *Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 25520, 9635–9640.
**Relevance:** Pioneering use of IAANS labeling to probe the single Ca²⁺-specific regulatory site of cardiac TnC. Demonstrates ~10-fold increase in affinity when cTnC is complexed with TnI ([4.1], [4.2], [4.3]).
**Methodology Note:** IAANS fluorescence titration.
**Flags/Caveats:** Ambient temperature; numeric Ka/Kd values were garbled in OCR text. Species not explicitly stated in accessible pages.

### Paper 5: Dong et al. 1996
**Citation:** Dong, W.-J., Rosenfeld, S.S., Wang, C.-K., Gordon, A.M., & Cheung, H.C. 1996. Kinetic studies of calcium binding to the regulatory site of troponin C from cardiac muscle. *Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 2712, 688–694.
**Relevance:** Reports pKd = 5.78 (Kd ≈ 1.66 µM) for the regulatory site of IAANS-labeled C35S cTnC mutant at 20°C, plus kinetic data at 4°C and 20°C ([5.1], [5.2], [5.3], [5.4], [5.5], [5.6], [5.7], [5.8], [5.9], [5.10]).
**Methodology Note:** IAANS fluorescence titration and stopped-flow kinetics.
**Flags/Caveats:** Recombinant cTnC (C35S mutant); 2 mM Mg²⁺ present; isolated cTnC only.

### Paper 6: Dong et al. 1997 — Kinetic Model
**Citation:** Dong, W.-J., Wang, C.-K., Gordon, A.M., Rosenfeld, S.S., & Cheung, H.C. 1997. A kinetic model for the binding of Ca²⁺ to the regulatory site of troponin from cardiac muscle. *Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 27231, 19229–19235.
**Relevance:** Provides Keq values at 4°C for isolated cTnC, binary complex (cTnC-cTnI), reconstituted troponin, and phosphorylated forms. Keq for cTnC = 3.2 × 10⁵ M⁻¹; binary = 49.0 × 10⁵ M⁻¹; whole Tn = 47.3 × 10⁵ M⁻¹ ([6.1], [6.2], [6.3], [6.4]).
**Methodology Note:** IAANS stopped-flow kinetics and fluorescence titration.
**Flags/Caveats:** pH 7.2, 0.3 M KCl, 3 mM Mg²⁺, 4°C. Species not explicitly stated.

### Paper 7: Dong et al. 1997 — Biophysical Journal
**Citation:** Dong, W.-J., Wang, C.-K., Gordon, A.M., & Cheung, H.C. 1997. Disparate fluorescence properties of IAANS attached to Cys-84 and Cys-35 of troponin C in cardiac muscle troponin. *Biophysical Journal*, 722, 850–857.
**Relevance:** Reports Ka values (× 10⁻⁵ M⁻¹) for IAANS-labeled cTnC in multiple complex states (isolated, binary, ternary, phosphorylated) under both EGTA and Mg²⁺ conditions at 20°C ([7.1], [7.2], [7.3]).
**Methodology Note:** IAANS fluorescence titration with nonlinear least-squares fitting.
**Flags/Caveats:** EGTA = 1 mM, Mg²⁺ = 3 mM; 20°C; basic buffer pH 7.2.

### Paper 8: Ogawa 1985
**Citation:** Ogawa, Y. 1985. Calcium binding to troponin C and troponin: effects of Mg²⁺, ionic strength and pH. *Journal of Biochemistry*, 974, 1011–1023.
**Relevance:** Comprehensive study of Ca²⁺ binding to TnC and troponin using metallochromic indicator at 20°C, reporting Ka values for high- and low-affinity sites and Mg²⁺ competition constants ([8.1], [8.2], [8.3], [8.4]).
**Methodology Note:** Metallochromic indicator (tetramethylmurexide) and stopped-flow kinetics.
**Flags/Caveats:** Species source not explicitly stated in accessible text; pH 6.80.

### Paper 9: Tobacman & Sawyer 1990
**Citation:** Tobacman, L.S., & Sawyer, D. 1990. Calcium binds cooperatively to the regulatory sites of the cardiac thin filament. *Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 2652, 931–939.
**Relevance:** Demonstrates cooperative Ca²⁺ binding to reconstituted bovine cardiac thin filaments using IAANS-labeled cTnC, reporting Kapp = 1.2 × 10⁶ M⁻¹ with Hill coefficient ~1.9 ([9.1], [9.2]).
**Methodology Note:** IAANS fluorescence and ⁴⁵Ca ultracentrifugation.
**Flags/Caveats:** 25°C, 3 mM MgCl₂, pH 7.06.

### Paper 10: Robertson et al. 1982
**Citation:** Robertson, S.P., Johnson, J.D., Holroyde, M.J., Kranias, E.G., Potter, J.D., & Solaro, R.J. 1982. The effect of troponin I phosphorylation on the Ca²⁺-binding properties of the Ca²⁺-regulatory site of bovine cardiac troponin. *Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 2571, 260–263.
**Relevance:** Demonstrates that PKA phosphorylation of bovine cardiac TnI decreases Ca²⁺ affinity of the regulatory site by 0.21 pCa units, measured by IAANS fluorescence in the presence of 3 mM Mg²⁺ ([10.1]).
**Methodology Note:** IAANS fluorescence Ca²⁺ titration of reconstituted TnIA complexes.
**Flags/Caveats:** Temperature not specified in accessible text; bovine cardiac troponin.

### Paper 11: Tikunova & Davis 2004
**Citation:** Tikunova, S.B., & Davis, J.P. 2004. Designing calcium-sensitizing mutations in the regulatory domain of cardiac troponin C. *Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 27934, 35341–35352.
**Relevance:** Reports Ca²⁺ Kd (~7 µM) and koff (1263 s⁻¹) for the regulatory N-domain of human cTnCF27W at 15°C. Also reports Mg²⁺ affinity (1.9 mM) and mutant calcium-sensitizing data ([11.1], [11.2]).
**Methodology Note:** Trp fluorescence (F27W) and Quin-2 fluorescence; stopped-flow kinetics.
**Flags/Caveats:** Human recombinant protein; 15°C; 3 mM Mg²⁺; isolated cTnC N-domain construct.

### Paper 12: Hazard et al. 1998
**Citation:** Hazard, A.L., Kohout, S.C., Stricker, N.L., Putkey, J.A., & Falke, J.J. 1998. The kinetic cycle of cardiac troponin C: Calcium binding and dissociation at site II trigger slow conformational rearrangements. *Protein Science*, 711, 2451–2459.
**Relevance:** Provides kinetic parameters for site II of chicken cardiac TnC and reports macroscopic KD for sites III/IV ~ 0.2 µM determined by IAANS titration ([12.1], [12.2], [12.3], [12.4], [12.5]).
**Methodology Note:** IAANS fluorescence; magnesium green competition; stopped-flow kinetics.
**Flags/Caveats:** Chicken cTnC (C35S mutant); 4°C and 25°C; Mg²⁺ not specified in binding assays.

### Paper 13: Liou & Fuchs 1992
**Citation:** Liou, Y.M., & Fuchs, F. 1992. Pyrene-labeled cardiac troponin C: Effect of Ca²⁺ on monomer and excimer fluorescence in solution and in myofibrils. *Biophysical Journal*, 614, 892–901.
**Relevance:** Reports pCa ~5.8 for isolated pyrene-labeled cTnC and pCa ~6.5 for cTnC in the troponin complex using excimer/monomer fluorescence ratios ([13.1]).
**Methodology Note:** Pyrene monomer/excimer fluorescence.
**Flags/Caveats:** 5 mM MgCl₂; temperature not specified in accessible text; species not explicit.

### Paper 14: McAuliffe et al. 1990
**Citation:** McAuliffe, J.J., Gao, L.Z., & Solaro, R.J. 1990. Changes in myofibrillar activation and troponin C Ca²⁺ binding associated with troponin T isoform switching in developing rabbit heart. *Circulation Research*, 665, 1204–1216.
**Relevance:** Reports pCa₅₀ values for rabbit cardiac myofibrils at 2 and 10 mM Mg²⁺ (e.g., pCa₅₀ = 6.25 at 2 mM Mg²⁺, 5-day; pCa₅₀ = 5.66 at 10 mM Mg²⁺, 22-day), reflecting Ca²⁺ sensitivity mediated through cTnC in the thin filament context ([14.1]).
**Methodology Note:** MgATPase activity pCa curves of cardiac myofibrils.
**Flags/Caveats:** 30°C, pH 7.0; data reflect myofibrillar Ca²⁺ sensitivity rather than isolated cTnC binding directly.

### Paper 15: Gillis & Tibbits 2002
**Citation:** Gillis, T.E., & Tibbits, G.F. 2002. Beating the cold: the functional evolution of troponin C in teleost fish. *Comparative Biochemistry and Physiology Part A*, 1324, 763–772.
**Relevance:** Compares Ca²⁺ sensitivity of salmonid versus mammalian cardiac TnC at 21°C and 7°C, reporting pCa₅₀ shifts with temperature and pH ([15.1]).
**Methodology Note:** Ca²⁺ sensitivity assays of isolated cTnC.
**Flags/Caveats:** Numeric Kd/Ka values not provided directly; pCa₅₀ shifts reported (Δ0.14 at 21°C, Δ1.35 at 7°C with pH change).

## Excluded Papers List

1. **Palmer & Kentish 1994** — Reports pCa₅₀ from skinned fiber force measurements; does not directly measure cTnC binding constants.
2. **Rosenfeld & Taylor 1985** — Primarily skeletal TnC; cardiac-specific data not clearly separable in accessible text.
3. **Reid & Hodges 1980** — Theoretical/hypothesis paper on cooperativity, not primary experimental binding data.
4. **Negele et al. 1992** — Reports stoichiometry but no fitted Kd/Ka values in accessible text; included in table with partial data.
5. **Pan & Solaro 1987** — Reports ⁴⁵Ca washout kinetics in skinned fibers rather than equilibrium binding constants; included with partial data.
6. **Putkey et al. 1989** — Site-directed mutagenesis of cTnC; pCa curves in skinned fibers but no explicit Kd/Ka for isolated protein in accessible text.
7. **Zhang et al. 1995** — Focuses on phosphorylation effects on force/pCa sensitivity rather than direct binding constants for cTnC.
8. **Johnson et al. 1994** — Modulation of Ca²⁺ exchange; primarily skeletal TnC with some cardiac comparisons.
9. **Yamada 2004** — ITC study; primarily skeletal TnC and calmodulin; cardiac TnC data minimal.
10. **Davis et al. 2002** — Engineering Mg²⁺ binding into skeletal TnC; cardiac TnC data referenced but not primary focus.
11. **Kleerekoper & Putkey 1999** — Drug binding to cTnC; Ca²⁺ binding data is secondary/indirect.

## Confidence Assessment
