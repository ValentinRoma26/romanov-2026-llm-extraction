# Cardiac Troponin C Calcium Binding Kinetics: Systematic Literature Review (Pre-2005)

## Overview

This systematic review identified **15 primary research articles** published before January 1, 2005, that report experimentally derived quantitative calcium-binding metrics for cardiac troponin C (cTnC). Data were extracted across multiple species (bovine, chicken, rabbit, human recombinant, dog), temperatures (4–25°C), magnesium concentrations (0–10 mM), protein states (isolated cTnC, binary complexes, whole troponin, reconstituted thin filaments, and skinned fibers), and methodologies (equilibrium dialysis, IAANS fluorescence, Trp fluorescence, CD spectroscopy, metallochromic indicator).

### Paper 1: Holroyde et al. 1980

**Citation:** Holroyde, M. J., Robertson, S. P., Johnson, J. D., Solaro, R. J., & Potter, J. D. 1980. The calcium and magnesium binding sites on cardiac troponin and their role in the regulation of myofibrillar adenosine triphosphatase. *Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 25524, 11688–11693.

**Relevance:** This landmark paper provides the most comprehensive equilibrium dialysis dataset for bovine cardiac TnC, TnI·TnC binary complex, reconstituted troponin, and native troponin, with and without Mg²⁺. It reports association constants for both high-affinity Ca²⁺-Mg²⁺ sites (Sites III & IV) and the low-affinity Ca²⁺-specific site (Site II) ([1.1], [1.2], [1.3]).

**Methodology Note:** Equilibrium dialysis with ⁴⁵Ca at pH 7.0.

**Flags/Caveats:** Temperature stated as 4°C in Methods. Some OCR artifacts in original text.
