# Cardiac Troponin C Calcium Binding Kinetics: Systematic Literature Review (Pre-2005)

## Overview

This systematic search identified **14 primary research papers** published before January 1, 2005, that report experimentally derived quantitative calcium-binding metrics for cardiac troponin C (cTnC). Data were extracted from equilibrium dialysis, fluorescence titration (IAANS, Trp, tyrosine), and stopped-flow kinetic measurements across multiple species, temperatures, magnesium concentrations, and protein complex states.

## Excluded Papers List

1. **Leavis et al. 1978** — Rejected: Rabbit skeletal TnC only; no cardiac TnC data.
2. **Iida 1979** — Rejected: Potentiometric titration of skeletal TnC; no cardiac data explicitly.
3. **Reid & Hodges 1980** — Rejected: Theoretical/computational hypothesis paper, not primary experimental.
4. **Weeks & Perry 1978** — Rejected: Characterization of TnC-TnI interaction regions; no binding constant reported.
5. **Head & Perry 1974** — Rejected: Reports cardiac TnC isolation but binding constants not clearly delineated for cardiac isoform in available text.
6. **Kohama 1979** — Rejected: Reports binding capacities (mol Ca²⁺/mol troponin) but not explicit Ka/Kd values for cardiac TnC.
7. **Davis et al. 2002** — Rejected: Study focused on engineering Mg²⁺ binding into skeletal TnC, not cardiac.
8. **Davis et al. 2004** — Rejected: Study on skeletal TnC (chicken), not cardiac.
9. **Dotson & Putkey 1993** — Partially excluded: Reports stoichiometry of Ca²⁺ binding for mutant cTnC by equilibrium dialysis but explicit Ka/Kd values not extractable from available text.
10. **Negele et al. 1992** — Partially excluded: Reports stoichiometry at defined pCa values but Ka/Kd not explicitly given for WT cardiac TnC.
11. **Kleerekoper & Putkey 1999** — Rejected: Drug binding study; Ca²⁺ affinity not primary focus.
12. **Palmer & Kentish 1994** — Rejected: pCa₅₀ values from force measurements in fibers, not direct TnC binding assay.
13. **Ovaska & Taskinen 1991** — Rejected: Computational model, not primary experimental binding data.
