# Cardiac Troponin C Calcium Binding Kinetics: Systematic Literature Review (Pre-2005)

## 1. Paper Justification Blocks

### Paper 1: Holroyde et al. 1980
**Citation:** Holroyde, M. J., Robertson, S. P., Johnson, J. D., Solaro, R. J., & Potter, J. D. 1980. The calcium and magnesium binding sites on cardiac troponin and their role in the regulation of myofibrillar adenosine triphosphatase. *The Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 25524, 11688–11693.
**Relevance:** This foundational paper provides the most comprehensive early characterization of calcium binding to bovine cardiac troponin C in multiple states—isolated cTnC, TnI·TnC binary complex, and reconstituted whole troponin—using equilibrium dialysis with 45Ca. It demonstrates that cardiac TnC has three Ca²⁺-binding sites: two high-affinity Ca²⁺-Mg²⁺ sites and one Ca²⁺-specific regulatory site ([1.1]).
**Methodology Note:** Equilibrium dialysis using 45CaCl₂ at 4°C in 150 mM KCl, 20 mM MOPS, pH 7.0, with EGTA or EDTA to control free Ca²⁺ ([1.2]).
**Flags/Caveats:** Contaminating Mg²⁺ (~1 × 10⁻⁵ M) present in nominally Mg-free conditions. Positive cooperativity observed at high-affinity sites; Hill coefficients of 1.2–1.5 were reported.

---

### Paper 2: Johnson, J. D., Collins, J. H., Robertson, S. P., & Potter, J. D. 1980
**Citation:** Johnson, J. D., Collins, J. H., Robertson, S. P., & Potter, J. D. 1980. A fluorescent probe study of Ca²⁺ binding to the Ca²⁺-specific sites of cardiac troponin and troponin C. *The Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 25520, 9635–9640.
**Relevance:** First paper to use IAANS fluorescence to selectively monitor Ca²⁺ binding to the Ca²⁺-specific regulatory site of bovine cardiac TnC. Demonstrated that IAANS labeling does not alter Ca²⁺-binding properties and provided affinities for isolated cTnC and whole troponin ([2.1], [2.2]).
**Methodology Note:** IAANS fluorescence at ambient temperature (~22°C) in 10 mM MOPS/Hepes, 90 mM KCl, 2 mM EGTA, pH 7.0.
**Flags/Caveats:** Temperature described as "ambient" only.

---

### Paper 3: Robertson, S. P., Johnson, J. D., Holroyde, M. J., Kranias, E. G., Potter, J. D., & Solaro, R. J. 1982
**Citation:** Robertson, S. P., Johnson, J. D., Holroyde, M. J., Kranias, E. G., Potter, J. D., & Solaro, R. J. 1982. The effect of troponin I phosphorylation on the Ca²⁺-binding properties of the Ca²⁺-regulatory site of bovine cardiac troponin. *The Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 2571, 260–263.
**Relevance:** First direct evidence that PKA phosphorylation of cardiac TnI modulates Ca²⁺ affinity of the Ca²⁺-specific regulatory site on cTnC. Reports pCa₅₀ shifts from 6.70 (control) to 6.43 (phosphorylated) and increased Ca²⁺ off-rate from 14.5 to 21 s⁻¹ ([3.1], [3.2]).
**Methodology Note:** IAANS fluorescence titration and stopped-flow kinetics at ambient temperature in 90 mM KCl, 10 mM MOPS, 3 mM Mg, 2 mM EGTA, pH 7.0.
**Flags/Caveats:** pCa₅₀ values converted to K (µM) and Kd (M⁻¹).

---

### Paper 4: Dong, W.-J., Rosenfeld, S. S., Wang, C.-K., Gordon, A. M., & Cheung, H. C. 1996
**Citation:** Dong, W.-J., Rosenfeld, S. S., Wang, C.-K., Gordon, A. M., & Cheung, H. C. 1996. Kinetic studies of calcium binding to the regulatory site of troponin C from cardiac muscle. *The Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 2712, 688–694.
**Relevance:** Definitive kinetic study of Ca²⁺ binding to the regulatory site of recombinant rat cTnC monocysteine mutant (C35S) labeled with IAANS at Cys-84. Proposes a three-step binding mechanism and provides the first detailed kinetic model for cardiac TnC ([4.1], [4.2]).
**Methodology Note:** Stopped-flow IAANS fluorescence at 4°C in 0.2 M KCl, 30 mM MOPS, pH 7.0, 3 mM Mg²⁺.
**Flags/Caveats:** Keq derived from kinetic model parameters as well as from steady-state titrations.

---

### Paper 5: Dong, W.-J., Wang, C.-K., Gordon, A. M., Rosenfeld, S. S., & Cheung, H. C. 1997
**Citation:** Dong, W.-J., Wang, C.-K., Gordon, A. M., Rosenfeld, S. S., & Cheung, H. C. 1997. A kinetic model for the binding of Ca²⁺ to the regulatory site of troponin from cardiac muscle. *The Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 27231, 19229–19235.
**Relevance:** Extends the kinetic model from Dong et al. 1996 to reconstituted cardiac troponin and the cTnC-cTnI binary complex using cTnC(C84S) labeled with IAANS at Cys-35. Demonstrates ~15-fold enhanced Ca²⁺ affinity when cTnC is complexed with cTnI, and ~5-fold reduction upon PKA phosphorylation of cTnI ([5.1], [5.2]).
**Methodology Note:** Stopped-flow IAANS fluorescence at 4°C in 0.3 M KCl, 30 mM MOPS, pH 7.2, 3 mM Mg²⁺.
**Flags/Caveats:** Recombinant chicken cTnC mutant; high ionic strength conditions.

---

### Paper 6: Hazard, A. L., Kohout, S. C., Stricker, N. L., Putkey, J. A., & Falke, J. J. 1998
**Citation:** Hazard, A. L., Kohout, S. C., Stricker, N. L., Putkey, J. A., & Falke, J. J. 1998. The kinetic cycle of cardiac troponin C: Calcium binding and dissociation at site II trigger slow conformational rearrangements. *Protein Science*, 711, 2451–2459.
**Relevance:** Elucidates the kinetic cycle of calcium binding and conformational change at site II of chicken cardiac TnC. Demonstrates that Ca²⁺ binding/dissociation is kinetically separable from the subsequent conformational change. Reports site II KD = 2–5 µM at 25°C and 2–8 µM at 4°C, and sites III/IV KD = 0.2 µM at 25°C ([6.1], [6.2]).
**Methodology Note:** IAANS fluorescence and stopped-flow using Quin-2/BAPTA chelators at 90 mM KCl, 10 mM MOPS, pH 7.0, 4°C and 25°C.
**Flags/Caveats:** KD ranges given; C35S mutation shown not to perturb Ca²⁺ affinity significantly.

---

### Paper 7: Tikunova, S. B., & Davis, J. P. 2004
**Citation:** Tikunova, S. B., & Davis, J. P. 2004. Designing calcium-sensitizing mutations in the regulatory domain of cardiac troponin C. *Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 27934, 35341–35352.
**Relevance:** Characterizes Ca²⁺ binding to recombinant human cardiac TnC using an engineered Trp27 fluorescent reporter (F27W mutation). Provides wild-type cTnCF27W Kd(Ca) = 7 µM (no Mg) and 24 µM (3 mM Mg) at 15°C, along with comprehensive kinetic parameters and competitive Mg²⁺ binding data ([7.1]).
**Methodology Note:** Intrinsic Trp fluorescence, stopped-flow kinetics, and artificial calcium transients (ACTs) at 15°C in 10 mM MOPS, 90 mM KCl, pH 7.0.
**Flags/Caveats:** F27W mutation is not native; data represents isolated N-domain behavior of human cTnC.

---

### Paper 8: Stull, J. T., & Buss, J. E. 1978
**Citation:** Stull, J. T., & Buss, J. E. 1978. Calcium binding properties of beef cardiac troponin. *Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 25317, 5932–5938.
**Relevance:** Early comprehensive study of bovine cardiac troponin Ca²⁺ binding using equilibrium dialysis and gel filtration. Provides binding constants at multiple Mg²⁺ concentrations and pH values. Reports two Ca²⁺-binding sites with [Ca²⁺]₀.₅ = 0.43 µM in 10 mM Mg and ~0.025 µM without Mg ([8.1], [8.2], [8.3]).
**Methodology Note:** Equilibrium dialysis with Ca²⁺-EGTA or Ca²⁺-EDTA buffers, and gel filtration (Hummel-Dreyer), at 4°C in 150 mM KCl, 30 mM MOPS, pH 7.0–7.2.
**Flags/Caveats:** Reports a single class of high-affinity binding sites, without separating Ca²⁺-Mg²⁺ from Ca²⁺-specific sites—this contrasts with Holroyde et al. 1980. pH effects studied at 6.2–7.2.

---

### Paper 9: Pan, B.-S., & Solaro, R. J. 1987
**Citation:** Pan, B.-S., & Solaro, R. J. 1987. Calcium-binding properties of troponin C in detergent-skinned heart muscle fibers. *The Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 26216, 7839–7849.
**Relevance:** Measures Ca²⁺ binding directly in canine cardiac skinned fibers using 45Ca, providing binding data for cTnC in situ within the myofibrillar lattice. Identifies two physiological site classes (Class I: Ca²⁺-Mg²⁺, K₁ = 2.63 × 10⁷ M⁻¹; Class II: Ca²⁺-specific, K₂ = 9.87 × 10⁵ M⁻¹ at 2 mM Mg²⁺) ([9.1], [9.2]).
**Methodology Note:** 45Ca equilibrium binding at ~22°C in 60 mM imidazole, pH 7.0, ionic strength 0.15 M, ±ATP.
**Flags/Caveats:** Binding measured on entire skinned fiber, not purified protein; myosin contribution assessed separately.

---

### Paper 10: Tobacman, L., & Sawyer, D. 1990
**Citation:** Tobacman, L., & Sawyer, D. 1990. Calcium binds cooperatively to the regulatory sites of the cardiac thin filament. *The Journal of Biological Chemistry*, 2652, 931–939.
**Relevance:** Demonstrates cooperative Ca²⁺ binding to the single regulatory site on cardiac TnC when troponin is incorporated into reconstituted thin filaments. Reports Kapp = 1.2 × 10⁶ M⁻¹ with Hill coefficient of 1.9 for IAANS-exchanged thin filaments, and native thin filament binding at ~4 × 10⁵ M⁻¹ ([10.1], [10.2]).
**Methodology Note:** IAANS fluorescence titration of troponin-exchanged bovine cardiac thin filaments at 25°C, 20 mM imidazole, pH 7.06, 3 mM MgCl₂, 6.5–11 mM KCl.
**Flags/Caveats:** Cooperativity parameter Y = 3–4.2 indicates inter-troponin interactions along the thin filament.

## 3. Excluded Papers List

The following papers were encountered during the search but excluded for the stated reasons:

- **Hannon et al. 1992** — Measured pCa-fluorescence relations in skinned fibers reconstituted with cardiac TnC-IAANS; primarily reports pCa₅₀ for force/fluorescence rather than explicit binding constants for isolated protein.
- **Rosenfeld & Taylor 1985** — Focused on skeletal muscle TnC kinetics; cardiac TnC data not explicitly reported as binding constants.
- **MacLachlan et al. 1990** — Studied bepridil binding to cardiac TnC; reports drug-TnC affinity rather than Ca²⁺-cTnC binding constants.
- **Zhang et al. 1995** — Phosphorylation of cardiac TnI; primarily reports pCa₅₀ for force development in skinned fibers (functional assay), not direct binding to cTnC.
- **Kleerekoper & Putkey 1999** — Drug binding to cardiac TnC using NMR; Ca²⁺ affinity mentioned only in context of drug effects, no standalone Ca²⁺ binding data reported.
- **Davis et al. 2002** — Engineering competitive Mg binding into skeletal TnC; cardiac TnC used as comparison but primary data is on skeletal isoform.
- **Gillis & Tibbits 2002** — Review of teleost cTnC evolution; does not contain primary binding data (refers to unpublished data).
- **Karibe et al. 2001** — Reports binding constant changes due to tropomyosin V95A mutation; thin filament binding, not isolated cTnC binding constants.
- **Iida 1979** — Potentiometric titration study focused on skeletal TnC; cardiac TnC not explicitly included.
- **Ogawa 1985** — Calcium binding to troponin C and troponin; primarily skeletal TnC data; cardiac component unclear.
- **Burtnick & Kay 1977** — Reported bovine cardiac TnC binds 3 mol Ca²⁺/mol, but concluded all three sites had similar affinity, which was later shown to be an artifact; no standard binding constants reported in the format required.
- **Putkey et al. 1989** — Site-directed mutagenesis of cardiac TnC; primarily reports pCa₅₀ from IAANS fluorescence in reconstituted myofibrils (functional assay context).
- **Dong et al. (1997, Biophysical Journal)** — Disparate fluorescence properties paper; characterizes IAANS fluorescence behavior rather than reporting equilibrium binding constants.
- **McAuliffe et al. 1990** — Reports pCa₅₀ shifts associated with TnT isoform switching in developing rabbit heart; primarily myofibrillar ATPase data.
- **Hincke et al. 1977** — Circular dichroism study of hybrid troponin species; does not report quantitative Ca²⁺ binding constants.
- **Leavis et al. 1978** — Proteolytic fragments of skeletal TnC; not cardiac-specific.
- **Reid & Hodges 1980** — Theoretical/hypothesis paper on cooperativity; does not contain primary experimental binding data.

## 5. Confidence Assessment

**Search Completeness: Medium-High**

The search identified the major research groups responsible for cardiac TnC calcium-binding studies (Potter/Johnson/Solaro group, Cheung/Dong group, Putkey/Falke group, Davis/Tikunova group, Tobacman group, Kay group, Tibbits group) and captured the canonical primary papers in the field. The 10 papers with full data extraction represent the core quantitative literature on cTnC calcium binding.

**Data Gaps:**
1. **Gillis et al. 2000, Am. J. Physiol.** — This paper (comparing mammalian and salmonid cTnC temperature effects) was identified in the search but the full text was unobtainable. It would likely provide valuable temperature-dependent binding data across species.
2. **Li et al. 2002, Eur. Biophys. J.** — NMR kinetic studies of calcium and cTnI peptide binding to human cardiac TnC; unobtainable.
3. **Finley et al. 2000** — Mg-Ca exchange in cardiac TnC bound to cTnI; unobtainable.
4. **Hincke et al. 1978, Can. J. Biochem.** — Ca²⁺-binding properties of cardiac and skeletal TnC by CD and UV difference spectroscopy; unobtainable.
5. Several papers reporting pCa₅₀ values from skinned fiber/myofibril force measurements (e.g., Parsons et al. 1997, Ball et al. 1994) were excluded because they measured force-pCa relationships rather than direct Ca²⁺ binding to the protein, per the inclusion criteria.

**Key Observations from the Dataset:**
- The regulatory site II of isolated cardiac TnC consistently shows Ka values in the range of 10⁵ M⁻¹ (Kd ~1–10 µM), which increases approximately 5–15-fold upon incorporation into the troponin complex ([1.3], [5.2]).
- Mg²⁺ competition reduces Ca²⁺ affinity at the structural Ca²⁺-Mg²⁺ sites (III/IV) by approximately 3–5-fold at 4 mM Mg²⁺ but has minimal effect on the Ca²⁺-specific site II at physiological concentrations ([1.3]).
- PKA phosphorylation of cardiac TnI consistently reduces Ca²⁺ affinity at site II by ~2–5-fold, primarily through increased Ca²⁺ dissociation rates ([3.1], [5.2]).
- Competitive Mg²⁺ binding to the N-domain regulatory site was demonstrated for human cTnCF27W with Kd(Mg) of ~1.2 mM ([7.1]).
